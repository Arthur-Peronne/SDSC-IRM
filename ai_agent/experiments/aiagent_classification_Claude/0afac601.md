## Conclusion

(`b02b8293`, which failed cleanly and clearly), this one failed more mildly (delta -0.0333, per-seed
0.675/0.650/0.725 — not a collapse) but the training dynamics changed unexpectedly. On the se3+se4

own question: removing the redundant `se4` gate does NOT make `enc4`'s dilation more valuable — if

dilation was added to `enc3` (`9dcc9dfd`). Combined with `b02b8293`, both fusion attempts on top of
`ac5057cf` have now failed — receptive-field (dilation) and normalization (GroupNorm) mechanisms both
underperform on this leaner base despite being tied/near-miss on the older se3+se4 lineage. Working
hypothesis for the remaining trials: `ac5057cf`'s specific architecture (se3-only, enc4 dilation=1,
plain bottleneck_conv) may be closer to a local optimum for this pipeline than it looks from any single
component's individual test — further changes should be evaluated on this base directly and should not
be assumed to inherit results from the se3+se4 lineage.
