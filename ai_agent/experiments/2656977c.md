how every fine-grained verdict in this campaign should be read: deltas under
~0.03-0.05 (which includes most of the patience/noise_std/weight_decay results from
trial 8 onward) are NOT reliably distinguishable from batch_size=1 run-to-run noise
at this sample size. Only the large, clearly-separated results — the initial lr sweep
(baseline 0.65 -> 0.75-0.80), and dropout's consistent, large, repeated failures
(-0.04 to -0.15 across multiple configurations) — stand clearly outside this noise
band. Per experiment.yaml's fixed verdict rule, the ledger's champion stays bed745a0
(compared against its recorded value, not a re-estimated mean) — this is a
deliberate, appropriately conservative design choice, not an error, but the campaign
summary should report this variance finding prominently: the "best" config found is
real (the lr/schedule/noise_std improvements are large and robust), but its exact
rank among the top handful of trials is not resolvable at this replicate count.
