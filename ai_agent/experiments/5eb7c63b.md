## Conclusion
Hypothesis rejected, and asymmetrically: patience=70 is worse than patience=30
was, not better as the "more LR-decay steps" mechanism predicted. Keeping a
higher lr active for longer (patience_scheduler=14 vs 10) appears to introduce
genuine instability at this small bottleneck (batch_size=1, per dim=60's own
GPU-nondeterminism caveat), not just a slower-but-cleaner convergence. Patience
now bracketed cleanly in both directions — 30 too short (clean but worse
plateau), 70 too long (worse AND less stable) — with patience=50 sitting at a
real local optimum, same value as dim=60's own champion. Closing the patience
axis here: testing intermediate values (40, 60) would just be refining a already-
answered question and risks re-litigating one idea per the budget-discipline
guidance, not worth it given the clean bracketing already obtained. All 5 open
hyperparameters are now individually closed at the champion's exact values
(lr=6e-4, weight_decay=0, dropout_rate=0, noise_std=0, patience=50). Next:
replicate the champion 2x to check how much of its +0.0079-over-baseline margin
is real vs noise-floor luck, before deciding whether further exploration (joint
combinations, finer grids) is warranted.
