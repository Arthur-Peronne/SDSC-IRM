## Conclusion
Hypothesis clearly rejected, and surprisingly so: `lr=7e-4` (between the old baseline
5e-4 and the champion 8e-4) combined with `dropout=0.05` produced the second-worst
result of the entire campaign (0.828 -> 0.745, delta -0.083), far outside the noise
band. This rules out "any lr in the upper-mid range works about as well with
dropout=0.05" — the interaction is specific to lr=8e-4, not a smooth function of lr
in this dropout regime. Combined with trial 57e78778 (dropout=0.04 near-tying the
champion) and this trial (lr=7e-4 failing badly), the emerging picture is that
`dropout_rate` has a genuine, moderately wide good plateau (0.04-0.06) at a FIXED
lr=8e-4, but the lr axis itself does not have the same tolerance once dropout is
added — lr=8e-4 is a comparatively narrow requirement, not just "high enough". This
is a real, non-monotonic interaction between the two HPs, not simply "more of both
helps up to a point". Champion remains 3e07b08d. This closes out further lr
variations combined with dropout; remaining trials will focus on confirming the
champion combination's robustness (further replicates) rather than more new lr/dropout
grid points, since the interaction is now reasonably well mapped.
