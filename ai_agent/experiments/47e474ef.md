# Trial 47e474ef — AE3dAsymResSeparableV2 — CHAMPION

<!-- ===== written BEFORE the run (agent) ===== -->

## Hypothesis
In the baseline (20fa5d8e, lr=1e-5), the best epoch was 52/200 and the plateau
scheduler (patience//5=6) had already halved lr four times (down to 6.25e-07) before
the 30-epoch early-stopping patience expired at epoch 82 — the last ~25 epochs of
budget were wasted on an effectively frozen model. This indicates lr=1e-5 is overly
conservative for this architecture at latent_dim=60: convergence was slow enough that
the scheduler ran out of room to make meaningful reductions before stalling. I
increase lr by 10x to 1e-4 (still 8x below the dim=240 champion's 8e-4, a deliberately
intermediate step given batch_size=1 makes higher lr riskier for stability) to reach a
better optimum in the same epoch budget, predicting an increase in
avg_validation_R2_mean.

## Implementation
Single-field change in configs/autoencoder.yaml: lr 1e-5 -> 1e-4. All other opened
HPs unchanged (weight_decay=0, dropout_rate=0, noise_std=0, patience=30). No
architecture change.

## Training Dynamics
Much faster and cleaner than the baseline: val loss reaches 0.0142 by epoch 1 (vs
0.245 for the baseline) and is already below the baseline's final best (0.000927) by
epoch 8. No instability despite the 10x lr and batch_size=1 — no spikes, no NaNs. The
scheduler started reducing lr at epoch 28 (1e-4 -> 5e-5) and the actual best epoch
(37, val 0.000678) landed just after that first reduction, i.e. the run benefited from
a "warm then cool" profile: the higher lr got the model into a good basin fast, and
the first decay refined it. Early stopped at epoch 67 (patience 30 after epoch 37),
using less than half the baseline's 82 epochs to reach a better optimum.

## Conclusion
Hypothesis confirmed, strongly: avg_validation_R2_mean 0.6517 -> 0.7475 (+0.0958),
new CHAMPION. Mechanistically, lr=1e-5 was leaving performance on the table by
converging too slowly for the fixed epoch/patience budget — the model was still far
from its achievable optimum when early stopping fired in the baseline. At 1e-4 it
reaches a markedly better optimum in under half the epochs, and the train/val gap
also improved in relative terms (train R2 0.8612 vs val 0.7475, a tighter ratio than
baseline's 0.8347 vs 0.6517), and validation R2 std dropped (0.1708 vs 0.2155) —
more uniform per-patient reconstruction quality, not just a mean shift. This confirms
lr was the dominant lever at this latent_dim, not regularization. Next: test whether
pushing lr further (still below the dim=240 champion's 8e-4) continues the trend, or
whether 1e-4 is already near a local optimum for this batch_size=1 setup.
