# Trial 5722444c — AE3dAsymResSeparableV2 — FAILURE

<!-- ===== written BEFORE the run (agent) ===== -->

## Hypothesis
noise_std=0.0001 was the only regularizer to help so far (+0.0027), unlike dropout
(hurt badly) and weight_decay (neutral), because it does not tax model capacity the
way weight-space regularizers do. I test a 3x increase (0.0003) to see if the
denoising axis behaves like the lr axis did — more of a good thing helping further,
up to a point — while staying a full order of magnitude below the 0.002 value that
caused a catastrophic R2 -0.185 collapse in the dim=240 campaign (at a much lower
lr=5e-4, where the relative perturbation-to-signal ratio may have been different
anyway). Predicting either a further small gain (axis has headroom) or a
plateau/regression (0.0001 was already the local optimum for this mechanism).

## Implementation
Single-field change in configs/autoencoder.yaml: noise_std 0.0001 -> 0.0003, on top
of the champion's lr=6e-4. weight_decay=0, dropout_rate=0, patience=30 unchanged. No
architecture change.

## Training Dynamics
Noticeably noisier from the start than both the champion and 319dacea — e.g. epoch
4-5 climb to 0.0017/0.0017 (vs a smooth descent at 0.0001), epoch 28 spikes to
0.001473. The best val loss reached (0.000708 at epoch 42) never gets close to the
champion's 0.000570 — this run plateaus at a visibly worse floor throughout, not just
at the very end. Early stopped at epoch 72, the earliest stop among all lr=6e-4-based
trials, consistent with hitting a worse optimum sooner rather than a slower better one.

## Conclusion
Hypothesis resolved: FAILURE, and a large one (-0.0523, the second-worst result of
the campaign after the neutral baseline). The denoising axis does NOT behave like the
lr axis (smooth, forgiving diminishing returns) — it has a sharp cliff between 0.0001
(small win, +0.0027) and 0.0003 (large loss, -0.0523), a non-monotonic response
consistent with the catastrophic collapse (-0.185) seen at 0.002 in the dim=240
campaign: past some small threshold, the added input noise stops acting as a useful
augmentation and starts actively corrupting the reconstruction target relative to the
signal magnitude in this data (registered, ROI-cropped 3D MRI voxel intensities are
presumably in a range where 0.0003 is no longer negligible perturbation). Val R2 std
also reversed sharply (0.1191, worse than both the champion's 0.0676 and even the
neutral baseline's context) — confirming this is a genuine instability, not just a
smaller mean shift. noise_std=0.0003 is rejected; champion remains 319dacea
(noise_std=0.0001). The boundary between 0.0001 (works) and 0.0003 (fails badly) is
narrow and worth mapping with one intermediate point (0.0002) before moving to the
last untested axis (patience).
