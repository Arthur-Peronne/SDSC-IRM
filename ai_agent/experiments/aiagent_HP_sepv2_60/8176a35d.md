operating point regardless of the current train/val gap size — the earlier
hypothesis that a wider gap would let dropout help was wrong; the real story is that
this architecture at latent_dim=60 needs its full capacity, and any train-time
capacity reduction (dropout far more than weight_decay) trades away more val
performance than it recovers from reduced overfitting, at any schedule length tested.
